# THE GRAY PILGRIM'S CORRESPONDENCE — the 12-letter arc (DRAFT for owner approval)

> Wave 2, Train W2-A, S-tier #3. **No code ships until the owner approves this copy.**
>
> Mechanics recap: the arc advances ONE letter per week the hunter ENGAGED the
> Gray Pilgrim (56 flights / 7 days). Each slot has two variants — CLEARED and
> MISSED — so the arc reads coherently no matter how their weeks actually went.
> Un-engaged weeks get no letter (he passes unseen). Letters arrive by push 2–6h
> after the weekly window resolves ("A letter was slipped beneath your door. — G.P.")
> and collect in a Correspondence drawer. Letters 6 and 12 end in one-week
> side-tasks that unlock lore pages. At 10 lifetime clears his mask cracks
> (portrait swap) and a personal MONARCH gate opens — letter 12 seeds it.
>
> Voice: patient, ancient, wry, never cruel. He counts everything. He is not
> the System — he is older than it and slightly amused by it.
> Placeholders: {flights} = their verified count that week · {target} = the goal ·
> {name} = alias · {clears} = lifetime clears. Banned-word check: clean.

## LETTER 1 — "The First Crossing"
**CLEARED:** You met me on my first crossing of your sky, {name}. {flights} flights. I have walked past ten thousand doors and fewer than you think ever open. Yours did. I will pass this way again in seven days. Leave the light on. — G.P.
**MISSED:** You reached for me on my first crossing, {name}. {flights} flights of {target}. Most reach once and never again — the road forgets them politely. I do not think the road is finished with you. I will pass again. — G.P.

## LETTER 2 — "I Count"
**CLEARED:** Some believe I carry relics in this coat. I carry ledgers. {flights} flights this crossing — I wrote it down before your phone did. I have counted since before counting had a name. It is good to have something worth writing. — G.P.
**MISSED:** You stopped at {flights}. I counted. I always count. Do not mistake this for scolding — the ledger has no feelings. But I will tell you what I have learned in a thousand years of pages: the number that matters is never this week's. It is next week's. — G.P.

## LETTER 3 — "The Old Roads"
**CLEARED:** Before your city there was a hill, and before the hill, a stair cut by hands nobody remembers. I walked it when it was new. Stairs outlive their makers, {name}. {flights} flights — you are building something that will outlive the ache. — G.P.
**MISSED:** I passed a ruin this week that was once the tallest tower on any horizon. Its builders stopped one week too early and called it finished. {flights} of {target}. The difference between a ruin and a tower is always the same seven days. — G.P.

## LETTER 4 — "The Question"
**CLEARED:** A question, and I ask it only of those who have kept pace: why do you climb? Do not answer me. Answer it at the top of the next stair, where the honest answers live. {flights} flights. You are becoming difficult to pass unnoticed. — G.P.
**MISSED:** I asked a hunter once why he climbed. He gave me a beautiful answer and never climbed again. I asked another; she said nothing and out-walked me for a decade. {flights} this week. Skip the beautiful answer. — G.P.

## LETTER 5 — "The Ones Who Stopped"
**CLEARED:** I remember every hunter who kept my pace, and I confess I remember longer the ones who stopped. Not with judgment — with the particular ache of an unfinished page. {flights} more flights say you will not be one of mine. Prove the ledger right. — G.P.
**MISSED:** {flights} of {target}. I once walked beside a hunter for six crossings, and on the seventh her door stayed dark. I still carry her page. It is the lightest thing in my coat and the heaviest. Leave the light on, {name}. — G.P.

## LETTER 6 — "One Road, For Me" *(side-task letter → LORE PAGE I)*
**BOTH VARIANTS (shared):** A request, which I have made of nine hunters in nine centuries: walk one road for me. Not for souls, not for the boards. Before I cross again — one single day of 10 flights, climbed in one turning of the sun. Do this and I will tell you what I was before I was gray. — G.P.
*(Side-task: 10 verified flights in one day within the week → unlocks LORE PAGE I: "He was the first architect of the Tower — the one who built the hundred floors, and the only one who never climbed them.")*

## LETTER 7 — "The Mask"
**CLEARED:** You have begun to look at my face, {name}. I feel it through the paper. Yes — the mask. No, it does not come off. It wears thin, though, near those who keep pace with me. {clears} crossings you have matched now. It has begun to notice you back. — G.P.
**MISSED:** {flights} of {target}, and still you looked up as I passed — I felt it. Most look at the coat, the ledgers, the road. You looked at the mask. Careful, hunter. Things that are looked at long enough begin to look back. — G.P.

## LETTER 8 — "The Court"
**CLEARED:** You have met the Sovereign's court by now — the Sentinel that starves, the mountain that walks. They do not write letters. They were made after fear was invented; I was made before. That is the entire difference between us, and it is why I use the door. — G.P.
**MISSED:** The Sovereign's consorts asked about you. I told them nothing — your pages are mine, not theirs. But hear me: {flights} will not hold the attention of things that count in centuries. Give me a number worth defending. — G.P.

## LETTER 9 — "I Wait Now"
**CLEARED:** An admission, and I will deny it if the System asks: I no longer cross your sky on schedule. I cross when I believe you will be on the stairs. A thousand years of appointments, and you have made me early. {flights} flights. Do not tell anyone. — G.P.
**MISSED:** I waited this week. I do not wait — understand, waiting is a thing I unlearned centuries ago, deliberately, at great cost. {flights} of {target}. You are re-teaching me a dangerous habit, {name}. See that it becomes worth the danger. — G.P.

## LETTER 10 — "The Crack"
**CLEARED:** The mask cracked once before. One hunter, long ago, kept my pace for ten crossings, and on the tenth the porcelain sang like ice on a spring lake. She saw one eye. She never told anyone what color it was. You are at {clears}. The mask remembers ten. — G.P.
**MISSED:** {flights}. The mask relaxed this week — I felt the hairline seams settle back. It prefers hunters who miss; it has worn me undisturbed for an age. Disappoint it. — G.P.

## LETTER 11 — "Almost"
**CLEARED:** I have run out of parables, {name}. This is what remains: {flights} flights, crossing {clears}, and an old thing on an old road who is no longer certain he wants to stay unseen. One more true week. That is all the mask can bear. — G.P.
**MISSED:** So close to the end of my paper, and you give me {flights} of {target}. Very well. I have waited longer for less. But know this — I have never written a twelfth letter. My hand is not steady. Make it necessary. — G.P.

## LETTER 12 — "The Last Page" *(side-task letter → LORE PAGE II + seeds the Monarch gate)*
**BOTH VARIANTS (shared):** This is the last letter, because after it there is nothing left to write — only something left to see. One more road, walked my way: {target} flights before Sunday, and on the final day, open your door before dawn. Bring nothing. The mask and I will settle our old argument in front of you, and you will learn why the Tower has exactly one hundred floors and why I never climbed a single one. — G.P.
*(Side-task: clear a Pilgrim week + open the app before 6am local on the final day → LORE PAGE II: "He never climbed because the hundredth floor holds what he sealed there — and at 10 lifetime clears, the seal answers to YOU. THE MONARCH'S CROSSING gate opens: a one-week personal gate paying a Monarch-tier relic and the eternal Hall entry 'Monarch-Breaker'.")*

## Implementation notes (after approval — phase 1 = letters only)
- Letters as a `PILGRIM_LETTERS` constant array in app.js; arc index = count of resolved ENGAGED Pilgrim weeks (derived from the boss state's kill_count + a missed-weeks counter stamped at weekly resolve).
- Delivery: local scheduled notification 2–6h (random, seeded) after the weekly window resolves + a Correspondence drawer (new row in the Gray Pilgrim's boss sheet; badge until read). Numbers spliced from `flights_daily` for that week.
- Lore pages: two static pages in the drawer, unlocked by the side-task checks (both run on existing daily-total math — no sub-day queries).
- Phase 2 (later, per the plan): sealed friend-mail; portrait degradation at 3/6/9 clears (needs the 4 portrait variants from the owner's art pipeline); the Monarch gate itself.
