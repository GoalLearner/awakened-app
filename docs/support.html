Apple Health Data

Awakened may request permission to read Apple Health data such as steps, sleep, workouts, flights climbed, and active energy. This data is used to verify habit and dungeon progress inside the app.

Health data stays on the user's device unless otherwise clearly stated in the app. Users can manage Health permissions at any time:

  • iOS Settings → Awakened
  • The Apple Health app → Sharing → Apps → Awakened

Permission can be granted or revoked per Health category at any time. If permission is denied, habits that depend on Apple Health verification can still be completed manually in the app.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact support?

Email richmondcampano93@gmail.com. Use the subject line "Awakened Support".

How do I manage Apple Health permissions?

Open iOS Settings → Awakened, or open the Apple Health app and go to Sharing → Apps → Awakened. You can grant or revoke any individual Health category there.

Why are my habits not auto-verifying?

Auto-verification depends on three things: (1) Apple Health permission is granted for the relevant category, (2) the habit threshold is met for the device-local day, and (3) the app has been opened recently so it can read updated Health data. If verification still fails, double- check the relevant Health category is enabled in the Apple Health app and that the data has synced from your iPhone or Apple Watch.

How do I report a bug?

Email richmondcampano93@gmail.com with subject "Awakened Bug Report". Please include your device model, iOS version, app version (visible in Settings → About in the app), and steps to reproduce. Screenshots help.

How do I request data deletion?

Email richmondcampano93@gmail.com with subject "Awakened Data Request". Most data lives on your device — deleting the app removes it. For any synced data tied to your account, we'll process the deletion request and confirm by email.
