    SOULS

    The currency of the dungeon.

    Souls are the essence collected from defeated dungeon bosses. You spend them to engage new bosses — every fight requires a wager. Hunt wisely.

    HOW TO EARN

    • Daily login — +15 souls every day you open the app
    • Boss kills — souls awarded based on rank
    E-rank kill+50 souls
    D-rank kill+100 souls
    C-rank kill+200 souls
    B-rank kill+400 souls
    A-rank kill+800 souls
    S-rank kill+1,600 souls

    HOW TO SPEND

    Engaging a boss costs souls based on its rank:

    E-rank engage25 souls
    D-rank engage50 souls
    C-rank engage100 souls
    B-rank engage200 souls
    A-rank engage400 souls
    S-rank engage800 souls

    Souls spent on engagement are non-refundable. If you disengage without a kill, your souls are lost. Hunt only what you intend to defeat.

    Compound Effect Bonus
    Bonus XP that grows the longer your streak.
    HOW THE BONUS SCALES
    Day 1–7 +5 XP/day
    Day 8–30 +10 XP/day
    Day 31–90 +20 XP/day
    Day 91–180 +30 XP/day
    Day 181–365 +50 XP/day
    Day 366+ +75 XP/day
    XP doubles on weekends.
    Morning Routine — 10 habits
    The first hour shapes the day. Sleep, sunlight, intention, movement — consistent mornings compound faster than anything else you do, because they make every other hour easier. Earn this bonus daily by completing all 10 morning habits.
    Locked-In — 16 habits
    Morning Routine plus afternoon focus and evening discipline. The full cycle. Most people win one part of the day. Locked-In wins all three — the highest ROI a single day can produce. Stack on top of the Compound Effect Bonus to earn both bonuses on the same day.
    STREAK FORGIVENESS
    🛡️
    Streak Shields
    Earn 1 shield for every 14 consecutive days you complete a pack. Up to 3 stored per pack. When you miss a day, a shield is auto-used — your streak holds.
    🌙
    Honest Day
    Once per month per pack, mark today as planned rest. Your streak holds without consuming a shield. Tap the 🌙 Rest chip on an active pack strip to use it.
    🔄
    Resilience XP
    When a streak truly breaks and you come back, you earn bonus XP scaled to time away: +25 / +50 / +100 / +200 XP (1-3 / 4-7 / 8-30 / 30+ days).
