Some bosses take the whole week.

• Weekly step bosses: six new bosses, one for each rank, beaten by your steps from Sunday to Saturday. 35,000 steps for E, up to 70,000 for S. No hunt to start: they go down on their own when your week gets there.
• Weekly sleep bosses: four more, beaten by nights of 8 hours asleep. 4 nights for C, up to all 7 for S, any nights in the week. Needs sleep data from Apple Watch, Oura or a sleep app.
• Weekly bosses drop from their rank's whole relic pool at about twice the usual odds, with a small chance at a Sovereign regalia piece or a mythic.
• Floor 100 within reach: with the best gear the summit is a fair fight, and every shadow you bring makes it surer.
