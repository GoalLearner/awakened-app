Hunts with friends start by themselves.

• Friend hunts run themselves: every day, you and each friend who walks share a hunt, already running, sized to what the two of you usually walk. Win it together and you both get paid.
• The seven are earned: the seven top-tier looks are no longer sold. Each one is earned by a milestone, and a locked one shows what it takes.
• Every background is free: earn a card background and it is yours to wear, member or not.
• A new Wardrobe: your looks and your backgrounds, each on its own tab.
