Ascent battles, rebuilt.

• A new battle screen: you and your foe face off full-size, and the foe shows what it will do next. Every move it shows has an answer. Parry a heavy, reflect a curse, interrupt a charge. Bosses fight to a rhythm you can learn.
• Every move has its own motion. Hold a heavy card to wind it up; a full Surge unleashes your fighting style's ultimate.
• The Wardrobe grows: frames, inks and auras for your portrait and your name, every one earned.
• Pins: wear up to two badges under your name. 100K Step Club, Prestige, Million Steps, Shadow Army, Worldgate MVP, Best Day.
• Your card: tap your title to wear any Ascent title you've earned, and average steps now cover the last three weeks.
• The Worldgate counts your steps once you've opened the app on two days that week.
