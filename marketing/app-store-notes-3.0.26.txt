Burn calories, beat bosses.

• Six calorie bosses, E to S: burn 150 active calories in a day to beat the E boss, up to 1,000 at S. They drop the sleep bosses' relics, so you can earn those without a sleep tracker, and the S boss can drop a mythic weapon.
• Mythic drops for everyone: when any hunter finds a mythic weapon, every hunter sees it in Hunter Activity.
• A cleaner feed: shadows raised by hunters you don't follow share one row, so your friends' news stays easy to find.
• Invite a friend: when a friend joins with your code, you both get 500 souls.
• Your card from any rank: below the top 20 on the Steps board? Tap your own row to open your profile card.
