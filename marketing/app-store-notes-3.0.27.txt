Sharper strikes, armed duels.

• Sharper strikes: every blow has a new look: blade arcs, stabs, flame and lightning cuts, a claw when a foe hits you, and smoke when a fight ends.
• Your ultimate in duels: sparring an Echo or a friend, your Surge fills as you trade blows. When it's full, UNLEASH your ultimate. Your shadows and a maxed weapon's special attack join in too.
• Six calorie bosses, E to S: burn 150 active calories in a day to beat the E boss, up to 1,000 at S. They drop the sleep bosses' relics, and the S boss can drop a mythic weapon.
• Mythic drops for everyone: when any hunter finds a mythic weapon, every hunter sees it in Hunter Activity.
• Raid wins for everyone: when a team beats The Grinning God, every hunter sees it in Hunter Activity, with the MVP named.
• The Grinning God grows: the raid now asks 200,000 steps and 200 flights in 80 hours.
• Invite a friend: when a friend joins with your code, you both get 500 souls.
