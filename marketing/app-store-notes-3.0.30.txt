Full-body hunter cards, new boss-kill badges, and clearer Worldgate strikes.

• Hunter cards now show your whole look, head to toe, with your rank at your feet and your title beneath it. Tap any hunter on a leaderboard to see theirs.
• New boss-kill badges: every 100 bosses you defeat earns a better badge, from an iron shield at 100 to a crowned, diamond-set gold crest at 1,000.
• Recent Strikes on the Worldgate now show what each hunter struck with: steps, flights and sleep, each in its own color.
• The Friends tab on the Steps board now shows everyone's badges.
• A drop past its cap now sells for souls the moment it lands.
• The 200K and 300K weeks are now pins in the Wardrobe.
• Bloodmoon, Dawnbringer, The Third Blade, The Unbroken, Vanguard and Stardust Sovereign look sharper.
