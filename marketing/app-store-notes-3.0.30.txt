New boss-kill badges, Worldgate strikes that say what they hit with, and badges on the Friends board.

• New boss-kill badges: every 100 bosses you defeat earns a better badge on the Steps board, from an iron shield at 100 to a crowned, diamond-set gold crest at 1,000.
• Recent Strikes on the Worldgate now say what each hunter struck with: steps, flights and sleep, each in its own colour. Each hunter's circle wears their rank colour.
• The Friends tab on the Steps board now shows the same badges as This Week: the 100K, 200K and 300K badges and boss kills.
• A drop past its cap now sells for souls the moment it lands, so every drop pays.
• The 200K and 300K weeks are now pins of their own in the Wardrobe.
