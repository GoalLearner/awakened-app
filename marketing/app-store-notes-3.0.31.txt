A cleaner hunter card, five new pins, title colors, and a tidier Hunter Activity.

• Hunter cards are cleaner: your whole look on the left, your name, title and badges on the right, and your stats in one panel.
• Five new pins to show off: Mythics, God Slayer, Weekly #1, Anomalies and Everest.

• Stats keep earning XP past level 20, all the way to 200,000.
• Hunter Activity is organized: shadows, milestones, bosses, drops and health each in their own group, and every rare drop shows.
• The Worldgate's bars stop at their goal, and each week's goals adjust to how hunters are doing.
