Half-each duo bosses, and cleaner, calmer screens.

• Four new A and S duo bosses, two for steps and two for stairs. Each of you does your own half; one hunter's extra doesn't count for the other. They drop A and S pieces that rarely drop elsewhere, and the S pair can drop a mythic.
• Fewer words everywhere. The explainers and repeated labels are gone, so the numbers that matter stand out.
• The tabs you open every day hold still: no more pulsing and glowing in the background.
• Your Shadow Army lines up by rank, strongest first.
• Set bonuses show only the bonus you actually have.
• The Highest Floor leader card shows the leader's full name.
• What's New in the app catches up on everything since 3.0.29.
