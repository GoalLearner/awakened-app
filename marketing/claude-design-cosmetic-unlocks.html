ClaudeDesign brief — earning a look (avatar skins and card backgrounds)

What you're designing

The moment a hunter in Awakened EARNS a cosmetic reward. Two kinds, one family:

  1. AN AVATAR SKIN — a full character look the hunter wears on every leaderboard, profile card and co-op roster. Seven of them, the best art in the app. The heavier of the two moments.
  2. A CARD BACKGROUND — a wide painted scene behind the hunter's leaderboard row and profile card. Eighteen of them. The lighter moment.

Design the whole sensory moment: visuals, motion, sound and haptics, not only the graphics.

Why this matters

Until now the seven skins were sold for money. The owner is making every one of them earned only: no price, no shop. Each skin now stands for a real milestone (a 30-day streak, a million steps, S rank), so the moment it arrives has to feel like the payoff of weeks or months of real effort. Today nothing happens at all: a background quietly flips from locked to EARNED inside the Wardrobe, and most hunters never notice.

The app in one breath

Dark-fantasy "level up your real life" habit RPG with Solo-Leveling energy, driven by Apple Health (steps, sleep, workouts). Deep near-black navy backgrounds, Cinzel serif for heroic headings, JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers, gold (#f5b842 / #fbbf24) reserved for the biggest achievements, violet (#a78bfa / #8b5cf6) for the rare tier, hex / sigil / rune motifs, premium glow and particle work. It must feel earned and powerful, never cute, never childish.

Skins (portrait art, shown in a round frame; the ring colour is the rarity):

Backgrounds (wide banner art, about 3:1): The Hundred-K City (100K step club), The Summit (clear Floor 100), Stormbreaker (reach S rank), Beyond the Peak (S+ prestige), The Boneyard (500 boss kills), Champion's Ring (Arena tier), The Vault (own a mega-rare), The Eternal Flame (365-day streak), The Grinning Dark (defeat The Grinning God), the six rank gates E through S (clear every boss of a rank), The Full Vault, The Complete Log, Thirty Days Sworn (30-day walk or sleep streak).

When it fires — the cases you must cover

  1. One reward, landing right after the thing that earned it (a boss kill, an Ascent floor, the morning step sync, or simply opening the app on the 30th day in a row).
  2. A pair: some milestones award a skin AND a background at the same instant. Reaching S rank gives Dawnbringer + Stormbreaker.
  3. A backlog: on the first launch after this update, a veteran can be owed several at once (the top hunter is owed three skins and a handful of backgrounds). It must not become ten screens of the same animation.

Screens (iPhone, 390 × 844)

  1. Skin earned, legendary — the art, its name, the rarity, the milestone that earned it as one quiet line, and two choices: EQUIP (puts it on now) and a quieter way to keep it for later.
  2. Skin earned, mythic — same structure, clearly heavier: this is the rarest thing in the app.
  3. Background earned — the banner, its name, the milestone line, SET AS CARD and later.
  4. A pair or a backlog — your call how they chain or gather (one after another, a reveal that fans out, a closing "collection" beat). Each reward must still get its own moment of focus.
  5. Reduced motion — the same information with no movement.

Rules the owner has set (keep all of them)

  • TAP TO CONTINUE: celebrations never auto-dismiss; the screen waits, and says so with one quiet "TAP TO CONTINUE" line.
  • Minimal words. No explanations of what is self-evident, no instructions, no paragraphs. If a line can go, it goes.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, never the words "fell" or "felled".
  • Hunter names are lowercase and never set in Cinzel.
  • One blocking screen at a time: this moment waits its turn behind a boss result or a rank-up.

It must sit beside the ceremonies that already exist

The ARISE ceremony (black cut, a shadow's silhouette rises, the word ARISE slams in), the relic reveal (mythic relics get a mega-reveal with motes and a flare), the rank-up screen, and the Worldgate card (a glowing gate icon cracks open, a podium rises). Earning a skin should feel like its own family, as heavy as a mythic relic, and never heavier than reaching a new letter rank.

Attached

The seven skin portraits and four of the background banners (Stormbreaker, Thirty Days Sworn, The Summit, The E-Gate). Use them as the real art.

What to hand back

An HTML prototype that plays each case above, plus a short timing table: each beat's time, its haptic (light, medium or heavy) and its sound (a chime, a swell, a hit), so the motion, sound and touch can be built to match.
