ClaudeDesign brief — The Wardrobe, rebuilt for earned looks

What you're designing

The Wardrobe in Awakened: the sheet that opens when a hunter taps their own avatar. It is where they choose the look they wear and the background behind their card on every leaderboard. Redesign it as two tabs:

  1. LOOKS — the avatar a hunter wears.
  2. BACKGROUNDS — the painted scene behind their leaderboard row and profile card.

Three screenshots of today's Wardrobe are attached (current-1-looks, current-2-backgrounds, current-3-shop). Today it is one long scroll: the equipped portrait, a grid of free looks, the background grid, then a SHOP of seven paid skins at the very bottom.

Why it's changing

  • The seven top-tier skins are no longer sold. Each one is now earned by a real milestone. They are the best art in the app and today they sit at the bottom behind a price tag. The owner wants them up front, where a hunter sees what there is to earn.
  • Backgrounds are no longer a member perk. Every hunter can wear any background they have earned.
  • One scroll is too long. Two tabs keep each screen simple.

The app in one breath

Dark-fantasy "level up your real life" habit RPG with Solo-Leveling energy, driven by Apple Health. Deep near-black navy, Cinzel serif for heroic headings, JetBrains Mono for labels and numbers, gold (#f5b842 / #fbbf24) for the biggest achievements, violet (#a78bfa / #8b5cf6) for the rare tier. Hex, sigil and rune motifs, premium glow. Earned and powerful, never cute. The current screenshots show the existing language (the gold medallion with four gems, the tile grid, the mono labels); keep that family, improve it.

The equipped look, large at the top: the gold medallion portrait with the look's name.

The top-tier skins (7), first, above everything else. Round portrait, ring colour = rarity:

Each skin has three states, all visible at a glance: - Locked: the art is still visible but clearly not yet theirs. Tapping it opens the skin's detail (below). - Earned: tap to wear it. - Worn: marked as the equipped look.

The free looks (12), after the top-tier skins: Your Class (the hunter's class default, tagged CLASS), Warrior, Ranger, Mage, Assassin, Paladin, Merchant, Sage, Wanderer, Bloodmoon, Tempest, Frostweaver. All always available; tap to wear.

A locked skin's detail (opened by tapping a locked skin): the art large, its name, its rarity, one short line of flavour that already exists for each skin (e.g. "The first light, given a body."), and the milestone with the hunter's progress toward it. This is the one place the condition is spelled out, so it has to read instantly.

BACKGROUNDS tab

A grid of wide banner tiles (about 3:1), each showing the real art, its name and either EARNED or the requirement. Basic (the plain card, the default) first, then:

The Hundred-K City (100K step club) · The Summit (clear Floor 100) · Stormbreaker (reach S rank) · Beyond the Peak (S+ prestige) · The Boneyard (500 boss kills) · Champion's Ring (Arena tier) · The Vault (own a mega-rare) · The Eternal Flame (365-day streak) · The Grinning Dark (defeat The Grinning God) · The E-Gate through The S-Gate (clear every boss of that rank, six tiles) · The Full Vault (own all 3 mega-rares) · The Complete Log (complete the Collection Log) · Thirty Days Sworn (30-day walk or sleep streak) · Founder's Dawn (founders only, 20 exist).

States: worn, earned (tap to wear), locked (requirement shown; tapping explains it the same way a locked skin does, in the background's wide format). Show how a chosen background looks behind the hunter's card, at the top of this tab, the way LOOKS shows the equipped portrait.

Rules the owner has set (keep all of them)

  • Minimal words. No explanations of what is self-evident, no instructions, no "tap to…" hints. If a line can go, it goes.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, never the words "fell" or "felled".
  • Hunter names are lowercase and never set in Cinzel.
  • Nothing is for sale on these two tabs.

How it connects

A separate brief covers the celebration when a skin or background is earned. Its EQUIP button lands the hunter here with the new look worn. The two should feel like one system.
