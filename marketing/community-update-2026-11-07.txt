Week of Nov 7 - v3.0.23

THE CLIMB
1. Ascent battles, rebuilt. You and your foe stand full-size, and above its head the foe shows what it will do next. Every move it shows has an answer: a guard move or your Surge parries a heavy, a cleanse throws a curse back at it, any hit interrupts a charge. Bosses fight to a rhythm you can learn, and they change at half health. Tap the foe's badge to see what is coming and which of your cards answers it.

2. Surge and ultimates. Trading blows fills your Surge. Spend some of it to parry, or fill it and unleash your fighting style's ultimate. Every move has its own motion now; hold a heavy card to wind it up.

YOUR CARD
3. Pins. A sixth Wardrobe slot. Wear up to two badges under your name: 100K Step Club, Prestige, Million Steps, Shadow Army, Worldgate MVP, Best Day. Each one shows your real number, and other hunters see them when they tap your name. Tap a worn pin to take it off.

4. Frames, inks and auras. Three more Wardrobe slots, all earned: a frame around your portrait, an ink that colors your name on your card and on the high scores, and an aura of motes, embers, stormlight or stardust.

5. The card itself. Your portrait sits centered, the founder badge is gone, and tapping the title under your name lets you wear any Ascent title you have earned. Average steps a day now covers the last 21 days instead of all time.

LOOKS
6. The Shadowborn. A new mythic look, earned at S rank alongside Dawnbringer. The Seven are now the Elite Eight.

7. The Rising, redrawn. All nine Rising looks have new art, from a wolf huntmaster to a three-headed dragon king. Looks you already earned change with them, and a hunter's card now names the look they wear.

THE WORLD
8. No more memberships. The Grinning God raid and co-op hunts with no entrance fee are open to every hunter.

9. The Worldgate's two-day rule. Your steps strike the Worldgate once you have opened the app on two days that week. The MVP spots and the bounty follow the same rule.

10. Final standings on Sunday. The Steps board holds last week's results until 12 PM PST on Sunday. Your new steps keep counting, and the new board opens at noon.

11. Shadows everywhere. When a boss leaves its shadow, every hunter sees it in Hunter Activity. Your Shadow Army now lives on your Status tab, and boss wins in the feed show the boss's rank.

3.0.23 is on TestFlight now and heads to the App Store next. Everything under THE WORLD is already live for everyone.
