What people hate about other habit apps

1,101 one- and two-star App Store reviews from 7 apps, matched against what Awakened already does. Sunday, November 22, 2026.

The short version

Awakened already answers the biggest complaints: billing traps, ads, shallow gamification, dead communities and bad step counts. Those are free marketing lines.

The open gaps are about missed days and schedules: people quit when one missed tap wipes a streak, when a habit can't be "3 times a week", and when they can't fix yesterday. None of the 7 apps does this well.

The complaints, biggest first

1. Billing traps and paywallsAWAKENED WINS
Fabulous (about 70% of its bad reviews), Productive, Habitify, Finch, Habitica

Charged after cancelling, trials that quietly turn into several subscriptions, refunds refused, weekly pricing, price hikes on loyal users, a 3-habit free tier, ads on nearly every tap.

Awakened: free to play, no ads, membership only through the App Store (cancel in iPhone Settings, no web billing). Souls are earned, not bought.
2. Shallow game, nothing to chaseAWAKENED WINS
Habitica, Streaks, Pikmin Bloom

Rewards are only cosmetic, classes don't matter, no real fights, nothing left once collections are done, the app feels stale.

Awakened: real bosses powered by real steps, the Ascent tower, raids, the Worldgate, ultimates, relics with drop tables, and now Auto Hunts that steer toward what you haven't collected.
3. The community died or turned toxicAWAKENED WINS
Habitica (removed guilds and the Tavern in 2023), Finch

Accountability groups gone, solo players can't find a party, harassment reports ignored.

Awakened: friends, daily co-op hunts made for you, matched partners for players with no friends, raid teams, Team Battles, and a moderated Board with reports.
4. Step counts that don't matchAWAKENED WINS
Pikmin Bloom

Counts low or late, no Health or Watch sync, battery drain from always-on GPS, the screen has to stay on.

Awakened: reads Apple Health in the background. The best device each day counts (Watch included), and there's no GPS.
5. One missed day wipes everythingGAP
Streaks, Habitica, Finch, Productive, Habitify, and the #1 wish across all three readers

A forgotten tap, a dead phone or a sick day erases a long streak. There's no repair, no pause, no "missed" separate from "forgot to log", and the guilt messages drive people away.

Awakened: a missed day breaks the streak. Streak shields were removed earlier ("not worth it at this moment"), and rest days exist only for rotation routines. Worth revisiting: a weekly rest day or a 24-hour "fix yesterday".
6. Rigid schedulesGAP
Streaks, Habitify, Productive, Habitica

No "3 times a week", weekly habits show zero streaks and still get daily reminders, no custom end of day (night owls want 2 AM), no clock-time habits ("up by 8"), can't set a starting streak.

Awakened: no "X times a week" vow yet, and the day ends at midnight.
7. Lost data and broken syncMOSTLY COVERED
Streaks, Habitica, Productive, Habitify, Pikmin Bloom

Years of history vanish after updates, a new phone means starting over, the watch and phone disagree, and editing a habit wipes its history.

Awakened: cloud save restores on a new phone. iPhone only, so no iPad or Mac to drift apart. Keep testing that a vow edit never touches its history.
8. Clutter, pop-ups and naggingWATCH THIS
Fabulous, Productive, Habitify, Finch

A celebration screen on every check-off, unskippable animations, sounds with no volume control, guilt notifications that can't be muted, reminders after the habit is already done.

Awakened: has many celebrations and sheets too. A "quiet mode" (skip celebrations, silence reminders once done) would head this off before it turns into reviews.
9. Crashes and lagEVERYONE
All 7

Won't open, taps that don't register, slow launch animations, widgets stuck.

Awakened: keep the launch fast and the check-off tap instant. A slow first open is what these reviewers punish.

What I'd build next, in order

  1. Fix yesterday. For 24 hours after a day ends, let a hunter check off a vow they forgot to log. It's the most common wish, and cheap to build.
  2. "3 times a week" vows. The streak counts weeks, not days, and reminders stop once the week is met.
  3. One rest day a week. Optional: the streak survives one missed day per week. Your earlier call was to drop shields, so this one is yours to decide.
  4. Quiet mode. One switch in Settings that skips celebration screens and silences a vow's reminder once it's done.
  5. Night-owl end of day. Let the day end at 2 or 3 AM for people who work late.

Free marketing lines

No ads. No trial traps. Free to play.

Bosses fall to your real steps, counted from Apple Health and your Watch.

Hunt with friends every day, automatically. No party to organize.

The App Store description and screenshots could lead with these. Habitica players angry about the guilds' removal are an obvious crowd for co-op hunts and raid teams, on r/habitica and in reply to "Habitica alternative" threads.

Method: the most recent 500 App Store reviews per app (US store, via Apple's public review feed), keeping only 1 and 2 stars. Three AI readers grouped them into themes. Counts are estimates and themes overlap. Each app's 500 reviews span a different period: Fabulous and Finch are this autumn, Habitica and Habitify go back years. No money spent, nothing posted.
